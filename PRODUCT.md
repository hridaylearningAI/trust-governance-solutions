# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders and engineering leads at software vendors selling into the enterprise. They hit buyer security/compliance reviews (300-question spreadsheets, SOC 2 / ISO 27001 evidence requests) that pull engineers off product for months. The site's visitors are these vendors; the reports the product produces are read by enterprise buyers.

## Product Purpose

Trust Governance Solutions (TGS) scans a vendor's stack, prioritizes and fixes compliance gaps, and issues a buyer-ready compliance report with continuous monitoring — so vendors pass enterprise compliance reviews before they even start.

## Positioning

One assessment, reused by every buyer. A single continuously-monitored compliance report that enterprise buyers accept as-is.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Book a Free Gap Check (15 minutes, no obligation). Secondary: See How It Works.
- The line a visitor remembers: "Vendor compliance, handled."
- Belief ladder: compliance reviews are slow and repetitive → TGS scans, fixes, and attests in ~3 weeks → the resulting report is mapped to buyer standards and accepted as-is → monitoring keeps it live, so it works for every future buyer.
- Proof on hand: product-derived proof (85% less engineering time on reviews, 142 controls checked, frameworks mapped: SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, buyer checklists). No third-party testimonials yet.

## Brand Personality

Assured, clear, quietly technical. The site should feel like the report it sells: precise, calm, already done. Confidence through specificity (numbers, timelines, framework names), not hype.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-gradient SaaS landing pages; dark "cyber security" clichés (matrix greens, glow, fear-based copy); bureaucratic audit-firm stodginess.

## Design Principles

- Show the product doing the work: the hero sells with a live-looking compliance dashboard, the report section with the actual document.
- Numbers over adjectives — every claim carries a figure or a timeline.
- Calm navy-and-teal authority; white space and order signal the compliance rigor being sold.
- One page, one action: every section resolves toward the free gap check.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation, semantic landmarks and heading order.
